Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I post a job opening through The Ray HR?

You can contact us via the contact form or email us directly with your job details. Our team will guide you through the process and help list your opening effectively.

What types of recruitment services do you offer?

We offer end-to-end recruitment services including sourcing, screening, shortlisting, and coordinating interviews — all tailored to meet your business needs at a competitive cost.

Is your service limited to Tamil Nadu only?

While we primarily focus on job opportunities in Tamil Nadu, we’re open to working with companies and candidates across India based on specific requirements.

What industries do you serve?

We cater to a wide range of industries including IT, BPO, manufacturing, retail, and more. If you're looking for specific talent, we’re here to help you find the right fit.

How quickly can you help us find candidates?

Our typical turnaround time is 2–5 working days depending on the role and requirements. We strive to provide quality profiles in the shortest time possible.

Can't find the answer you're looking for?

If you have more questions about our services, job listings, or partnership opportunities, don’t hesitate to get in touch. We’re here to help!
